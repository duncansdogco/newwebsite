What We Do

Our Doggy Daycare.

Welcome to Duncan's Dog Co., a family-owned dog daycare business that has been caring for dogs since 2011.

Nestled in a cottage surrounded by acres of enriching woodland, we give dogs a natural, safe place to explore, play and thrive.

The trees create a natural canopy from the British weather: shade from the sun when we get it, and shelter when the rain arrives. Scent, sound, texture and changing seasons make each day naturally stimulating.

We are one facility, not a chain or a handover system. Our staff are with the dogs from collection all the way through to home time, keeping faces, routines and care familiar.
