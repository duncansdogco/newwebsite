What their day is like

Your dog spends the day in 10 acres of private woodland in Cobham, with room to run, sniff, paddle and play with friends they see every week. The team know every dog by name, and the afternoon ends with a proper rest before home.

Getting your dog to the woods

We collect from your door

Our own team does the driving, so whoever picks your dog up is with them in the woods all day. Dogs ride in people carriers, not individual cages, and you can message your driver in the portal on the days you are booked.

Or drop off yourself

The woods are just off the A3 and M25 in Cobham, so it is an easy drive in.

Questions from Horsley families

Do you collect dogs from Horsley?

Yes. Collection from Horsley (KT24) is available subject to route capacity and your dog's routine. Dogs come to our Cobham woodland via the A246.

How do dogs travel from Horsley?

Dogs from Horsley (KT24) travel to our Cobham woodland via the A246 in our people carriers, with the same team who look after them all day. We plan the routes every evening so no dog is in the car longer than it needs to be.

Can I drop off my dog from Horsley?

Yes. Drop-off at our Cobham facility works well for Horsley families, especially those commuting via the A246 or nearby Surrey routes. It is a straightforward drive to the site.
