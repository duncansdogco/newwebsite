Getting Started

How it works.

Every new dog starts with a proper conversation and introduction, so we understand their routine, confidence and suitability before they join the woodland group.

Local FAQs

Dog daycare questions for Esher

Do you collect dogs from Esher?

Yes. Collection from Esher (KT10) is available subject to route capacity and your dog's routine. The journey to our Cobham woodland takes around 10 minutes via the A244 or A3.

How long does the journey from Esher take?

Dogs travelling from Esher (KT10) typically reach our Cobham woodland in around 10 minutes via the A244 or A3. They travel in our bespoke people carriers with familiar handlers throughout.

Can I drop off my dog from Esher?

Yes. Drop-off at our Cobham facility works well for Esher families, especially those commuting via the A244 or A3 or nearby Surrey routes. It is a straightforward drive to the site.
