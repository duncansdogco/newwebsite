Questions owners ask

Is Duncan's Dog Co. licensed?

Yes. Our daycare is licensed by Elmbridge Borough Council and rated five stars, licence number LN/201800994. Boarding is licensed separately, LN/202400651.

Where is Duncan's Dog Co.?

In Cobham, Surrey, on 10 acres of private woodland. We collect from 25 areas across Surrey and South West London, including Wimbledon, Putney, Clapham and Wandsworth.

How much is dog daycare at Duncan's Dog Co.?

£65 a day for one day a week, £60 a day for two and £55 a day for three or more, with collection and drop-off included. Weekend days are £75.

What is the staff ratio at Duncan's Dog Co.?

One member of staff to six dogs. The licence standard is one to ten, and the higher standard needed for five stars is one to eight.

Is Duncan's Dog Co. open at weekends?

Yes, 365 days a year including bank holidays, from 7.45am to 6.30pm.
