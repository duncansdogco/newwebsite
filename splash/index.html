About SPLASH

Your dog's first swim

Your dog's first SPLASH session is a 1-to-1 taster, priced at £40. During the session your dog has the sole attention of the SPLASH team while we assess their confidence, ability and genuine enjoyment in the water.

Not every dog takes to the water and that is completely fine. The taster is designed to find out whether SPLASH is the right fit for your dog, while making sure their first experience is positive and stress-free.

If SPLASH is a good fit, we confirm next steps and get you set up for regular sessions.

Why swimming?

Good for body and mind

Swimming offers something a walk cannot. Low-impact, full-body, genuinely joyful. Here is what your dog gets from a regular dip.

Joint-friendly exercise
Zero impact on joints. Ideal for dogs with arthritis, post-surgery recovery, or any breed prone to joint problems.
Full body workout
Every stroke uses more muscles than a land walk. Strong core, strong legs, genuinely tired dog.
Confidence in water
A positive first experience builds calm around water for life. Especially valuable for nervous or cautious dogs.
Mental enrichment
New sensations, new environment, new challenge. Swimming tires a busy brain as much as the body.
Great for senior dogs
Older dogs who struggle on walks often thrive in the water. Supported movement they can actually enjoy.
Pure fun
Some dogs are simply born for the water. SPLASH gives them somewhere to be exactly that.

What's included

Your first session, covered

Exclusive to regular daycare dogs.

  • 1-to-1 swimmingYour dog has the full attention of the SPLASH team throughout. No distractions, no group pressure.
  • Full assessmentWe observe confidence, ability and genuine enjoyment. If SPLASH is not right for your dog, we will say so honestly.
  • First Swim Session ReportA written summary of your dog's session, their confidence in the water and our recommendations.
  • Photos and videosWe capture your dog's first swim so you can see exactly how they got on.

Safe and supervised

  • Qualified team. Our pool supervisors have completed relevant water safety and canine welfare training.
  • Life vests for every dog. Every dog wears a correctly fitted buoyancy aid. No exceptions.
  • Fully supervised. Your dog is never left unattended in the water. A qualified member of the SPLASH team is present throughout every session.
