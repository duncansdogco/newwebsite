About sleepovers

Not a kennel. Our home.

Dog sleepovers at Duncan's Dog Co. are exactly what they sound like — your dog stays overnight at our woodland facility as part of the family. They sleep in our home alongside the team, not in a run or a crate in a kennel block.

This matters because it means a familiar environment, familiar faces, and a familiar routine. If your dog already comes to us for daycare, a sleepover is a seamless extension of their normal day.

We handle sleepovers for holidays, work trips, weekends away, or any time you need peace of mind that your dog is genuinely well cared for. Boarding licence LN/202400651.
