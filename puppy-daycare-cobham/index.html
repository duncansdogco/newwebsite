About puppy school

Puppy-focused. Gently socialised.

Puppy School at Duncan's Dog Co. is run as a dedicated pathway with gentler exercise, shorter sessions, more rest, and the right kind of socialisation at the right pace.

We take puppies from 12 weeks old, once they have had their initial vaccinations. Sessions are puppy-focused in our woodland setting, with carefully chosen, well-socialised adult dogs involved where it helps puppies learn calm manners, confidence and appropriate play.

Everything we do is focused on building confidence, positive associations with new dogs and people, and the calm, happy temperament that makes for a great adult dog.

★Accepting puppies from 12 weeks old

Why we're different

Real-world confidence, built gently.

Puppies learn through calm handling, careful socialisation, familiar people and positive woodland experiences. We keep introductions gentle and matched to each puppy's age and confidence, including calm adult role models where that supports their learning.

Built into every day

Built for where they are.

Not a course. Not a programme. Just consistent, expert care woven into each puppy day from the start.

Socialisation

Positive, consensual interactions with puppies and suitable adult dogs — learning to read cues, play safely and build confidence with good role models.

Training

Reward-based methods, good manners, recall foundations and calm handling built naturally into the day.

Habituation

Gradual exposure to new sights, sounds and experiences so puppies build confidence without overwhelm.

Recall & independence

Off-lead confidence, safe woodland recall and learning to self-regulate with guidance from the team.

Routine & structure

A consistent rhythm helps puppies understand expectations and settle faster with the same calm approach.

Progress & support

Regular updates, honest guidance and help with questions about behaviour, training and settling at home.

Common questions

Puppy School FAQs.

How old does my puppy need to be?

We accept puppies from 12 weeks old, provided they have had their primary vaccinations and received veterinary clearance to socialise with other dogs.

Are Puppy School sessions separate from adult daycare?

Puppies have their own pathway, shorter sessions and gentler pace, but they are not kept completely apart from adult dogs. Carefully chosen, well-socialised adult dogs may join them to model calm manners and help puppies learn safely.

How long are the sessions?

Puppy sessions are shorter than full daycare days, reflecting the exercise and rest needs of young dogs.

Do you offer door-to-door collection for Puppy School?

Yes — collection and drop-off is included for Puppy School just as it is for regular daycare.

When does Puppy School transition to full daycare?

There is no fixed age. It depends on your puppy's development, confidence and readiness.
