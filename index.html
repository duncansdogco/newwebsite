Why Families Choose Us

The same team, the same woodland, every day

We are one family-run site in Cobham, not a chain. The person who collects your dog in the morning is part of the team looking after them all day, and the woodland is ours. Dogs get proper outdoor days with shade, shelter and small, well-matched groups.

Your customer portal

Every booking on one calendar

Every day your dog is booked in, past and upcoming. Your regular days are already in there. Tap Create a booking to ask for an extra day, and you get a notification on your phone the moment we approve it.

Talk to the team, and to your driver on the day

Anything you need to tell us goes in one thread we can both see, instead of texts and WhatsApps to different phones. On the days your dog is booked in, the driver on your route is in that thread too.

See how their day went

Photos from the woodland and a report card, saved on your dog's profile alongside their vaccination dates and care notes.

Invoices that pay themselves

One Direct Debit through GoCardless, set up once. Every invoice stays in the portal with the PDF to download, and you can see at a glance what is paid.

Your details, kept current

Your contact details, emergency contacts and your dog's records, updated by you and seen by the team the moment you save.
