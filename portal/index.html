Your customer portal

Your dog's week with us

When you open the portal, you'll see the days your dog is with us this week and whether each one is confirmed. If you'd like an extra day, tap Daycare and pick the date, and we'll let you know as soon as it's confirmed.

Send us a message any time

Everything you send comes to one chat that the whole team can see, so there's no texting different phones. On the days we're collecting your dog, your driver joins the chat too, which is handy if you're running late or the gate code has changed.

We'll let you know when they're collected and when they're home

Your phone pings when your driver collects your dog and again when they're back home. We'll also let you know when new photos go up, when we've sent you a message and when we've confirmed a booking. Add the portal to your home screen and the notifications come through like any other app.

See what they got up to today

We add photos from the woods during the day, and a report card telling you how they got on. Everything is saved on your dog's profile, along with their vaccination dates and care notes.

Monthly invoices, paid by Direct Debit

You set up a Direct Debit with GoCardless once, and each invoice is paid from it automatically. Your statement shows every invoice and payment, a bit like a bank statement, so you can always see what's been paid.

Keep your details up to date

Tap your initials to see your past bookings, update your contact details and emergency contacts, and choose which notifications you get. When you save a change, the team sees it straight away.

Message your driver on the day

Our own daycare staff do the driving. On the morning of a booked day, the driver on your route joins your thread in the portal alongside the team. If you're running late, the gate code has changed or there's a bag of food to bring in, just write it in the chat. Your driver sees it on their route, and the office sees it too.

Drivers only hear from the families on that day's route, and your conversation with the team carries on in the same thread as normal.

Add it to your phone

Open the portal in Safari or Chrome, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app from then on and you get notifications for confirmations and messages, without installing anything.

Open the customer portal

First time here?

Every customer has been sent a sign up link by email. Your details, your dog's details and your emergency contacts are already in the portal, so you only need to set a password. If you cannot find the email, call 07731 798 899 or email info@duncansdogco.com and we will send it again.

Built here, at Duncan's Dog Co.

The portal runs on Genera, the dog daycare software we built at Duncan's Dog Co. Jess and Duncan started it to run this daycare, and it now runs bookings, invoicing, drivers and messaging for daycares, dog walkers, groomers and boarding kennels across the UK.

If you spot something that could be better, tell us. Your portal runs on the same software as every other business using Genera, so anything you report gets fixed for all of them.

FAQs

Questions about the portal

Here are the questions families ask us most often.

Still got a question? Call 07731 798 899, WhatsApp us or email info@duncansdogco.com.

Do I need to download an app?

No. The portal works in any browser on your phone or computer. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with notifications, and there is nothing to install.

Can I message the driver at any time?

You can message the daycare team at any time. Your driver joins the thread on the days your dog is booked in, so the note goes to the person actually on your route that day. On other days it goes to the office.

Who sees my messages?

The Duncan's Dog Co. team, and on booked days the driver on your route. Every reply is signed by the person who sent it, so you always know who you are talking to.

I am not a customer yet. Can I see the portal?

The portal is for families whose dogs come to the woodland. Once your dog has done their meet and greet and trial day, we send you a sign up link and your details are already in there.
